# Sonics Quit Tracker

A nicotine-quit tracker that builds the (what-if) Seattle SuperSonics from a terrible expansion team
into a contender. Every clean day you check in, the Sonics play one game. As clean days stack up, the
front office quietly makes better trades, hires and signings — you only find out after they happen.

## Decisions so far (Oct 21, 2026)
- 82-game seasons, one game per check-in day; short playoffs + offseason, then the next season.
- Improvement is paced by clean days (~day 365 = good coach, an All-Star, lots of wins).
- Daily check-in button. A slip = lose that day's game and maybe one recent improvement; keep the team.
- Real players, coaches and GMs only. No drafts, no made-up players.
- Real phone notifications (planned last).
- Future moves are hidden, even from the owner. Don't show the future player/coach/GM pool.

## Data
- `data/day1.json` — Day-1 roster, coach and GM, with sources. Snapshot of Oct 21, 2026.
- Seattle has no NBA team yet (expansion only being explored as of Oct 2026); the franchise is a what-if.
